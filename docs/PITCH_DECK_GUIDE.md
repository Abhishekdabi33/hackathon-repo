# 🎯 Hackathon Pitch Deck & Presentation Guide

## The 3-Minute Pitch Framework (Winning Structure)

Judges evaluate dozens of projects in a short period. Clear communication beats complex explanations.

---

### Slide 1: The Problem (30 seconds)
- **The Hook**: What real, painful problem does your target audience face?
- **The Metric**: Quantify the cost (e.g., "$X billion lost", "Y hours wasted weekly").
- **The Gap**: Why existing solutions fall short.

### Slide 2: The Solution & Value Proposition (30 seconds)
- **Your Concept**: What have you built? (One crisp sentence).
- **The 'Secret Sauce'**: Why is your approach uniquely effective? (e.g., local AI privacy, speed, domain fine-tuning).

### Slide 3: Live Demo / Product Walkthrough (60 seconds)
- **Show, Don't Tell**:
  1. Trigger the user flow.
  2. Show the real-time AI processing.
  3. Show the impactful end result.
- *Rule of thumb: Always have a 30-second screen recording backup in case the live demo glitches!*

### Slide 4: Technical Architecture & Innovation (30 seconds)
- Mention your stack: React + FastAPI + PyTorch / Fine-tuned LLM.
- Highlight technical hurdles solved (e.g., memory-efficient LoRA on 4GB GPU, hybrid local-cloud fallback, sub-100ms response time).

### Slide 5: Business Impact, Future Scope & Team (30 seconds)
- Who will pay for this?
- Next 3 milestones (e.g., mobile app, enterprise integration, multi-agent support).
- Introduce your 4 team members and their roles.

---

## Standard Hackathon Judging Rubric
1. **Innovation & Originality (25%)**: Is the concept fresh or a unique angle on a known problem?
2. **Technical Execution & Feasibility (25%)**: Does the code actually run? Is the architecture sound?
3. **Impact & Utility (25%)**: Does this solve a genuine problem with measurable benefit?
4. **Presentation & UI/UX (25%)**: Is the design polished? Was the pitch clear and convincing?
